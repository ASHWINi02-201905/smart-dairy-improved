Farm-to-customer journey

  1. Milk collection

    Milk is collected for processing.

  2. Quality checks

    Milk is checked before it moves on to processing.

  3. Processing

    Milk is processed into our dairy products.

  4. Packaging

    Products are packed for delivery.

  5. Cold storage

    Packed products are kept chilled.

  6. Delivery

    Orders are delivered to the customer.
