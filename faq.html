Frequently asked questions

How does daily delivery work?Staff record each delivery and you can see it in your account.
Can I choose how often I am billed?Yes – choose daily, weekly or monthly billing from your account dashboard.
How am I billed?According to your billing preference (daily, weekly or monthly), for delivered quantities at the price on each date, minus payments.
How do I report a missing delivery?Contact support using the details below.
